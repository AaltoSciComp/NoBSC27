Nordic Basic Scientific Computing 2027

Nordic Basic Scientific Computing is a gathering of those interested in supporting {scientific,research} {computing,data} (and a celebration of the diverse work that makes it all possible).

We gather for practical discussion and experience-sharing in the world of teams supporting {computing, data, and software}. It’s a time to roll up our sleeves, look under the hood and talk about what we really do each day (and not just advertise how good we are).

You can find plenty of conferences where people talk about how big their computers are, or how they should support researcher with technology, or how nice their citations are. You can find plenty of “AI” that don’t talk say anything concrete. But there’s some intersection, a “full-stack” view from the research support to the infrastructure. “AI” support actually means all the computing, data, ethics, infrastructure, and operational support needed to put all these pieces together [note: So is this an AI meetup? No and yes. “No”, because we aren’t talking “AI” buzzwords. “Yes”, because most of us are focused on supporting “AI” as well as possible.].

Also, to the best of our knowledge, there are few Nordic-scoped conferences designed for skill development and professional networking of this broad intersection of professionals [NeIC conference?: The NeIC conference was like this, but for NeIC staff and unfortunately won’t continue. We are trying to continue with that spirit.].

Basic info:

  • Aalto University campus, Helsinki [note: actually Otaniemi, Espoo, but I bet either you come from far enough away it doesn’t matter, or near enough you already know]

  • 2027 February 10-12 (Wed-Fri), roughly “lunch to lunch”.

  • CSC Ambassador’s meet-up on the Tuesday before.

  • If you would like space for other satellite meetings on the morning before / afternoon after, let the organizers know. Our spaces are reserved during this time and professional attendees can use them for satellite meetings.
